## Purpose

AtlasVanguard treats open interest as a structural indicator rather than a speculative signal. The objective is to map participation, liquidity concentration, and the changing shape of the market as instruments mature.

## Reporting approach

The report stack is intended to be:

1. Transparent about definitions.
2. Reproducible in future revisions.
3. Easy to compare across time windows and asset groups.

## Presentation layer

Charts are rendered as static images so they can be archived and cited without depending on client-side code. This keeps the public research portal aligned with institutional publishing norms.
